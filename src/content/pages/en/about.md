## Our story

[ABOUT_STORY] SK LABS is a cloud, DevOps, security and AI consultancy based in the Netherlands. Alongside client work we build our own SaaS products.

## How we approach work

- **Outcomes first.** We start from what you need to achieve, not from a favourite tool.
- **Everything as code.** Infrastructure, pipelines and policies live in version control so they can be reviewed, repeated and handed over.
- **Security from the start.** Identity, data and compliance are designed in, not added at the end.
- **Plain communication.** Clear trade-offs, honest estimates, no hype.

## Values

- **Craft.** We care about how things are built and documented.
- **Transparency.** You see what we do and why.
- **Ownership.** We aim to leave your team more capable, not more dependent.
