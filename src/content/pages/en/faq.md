description: Answers to common questions about SK LABS services, how we work with clients, security and data, and AI.
faqGroups:
  - title: Services and offerings
    items:
      - q: What does SK LABS do?
        a: We help teams build, secure and run cloud platforms and AI systems. Our work covers cloud infrastructure and migration, managed services, DevOps and platform engineering, cloud security and compliance, and AI and agentic AI consulting. We are based in the Netherlands and design EU-first where we can.
      - q: Which cloud platforms do you work with?
        a: AWS, Azure and Google Cloud. We recommend a multi-cloud setup only when there is a clear reason for it.
      - q: Do you work with regulated organisations?
        a: Yes, including finance and government. We design for requirements such as GDPR, NIS2 and DORA from the start, and we are clear about what we can and cannot cover.
      - q: Do you only advise, or do you also build and run things?
        a: Both. We assess and design, build the result as code together with your team, and can operate it afterwards as a managed service. You can use one step or all of them.
      - q: Can you tailor your work to our situation?
        a: Yes. Every engagement is scoped around your environment, constraints, data sources and regulatory needs. We start with a short assessment so the plan fits your reality rather than a template.
  - title: Working with us
    items:
      - q: What does a typical engagement look like?
        a: We assess where you are, design the target setup with clear trade-offs, build it as code with your team, and then operate it or hand it over. You can start with an assessment, a defined project or an ongoing retainer.
      - q: How long does a project take?
        a: '[TYPICAL_TIMELINES] It depends on scope. Assessments are short and fixed in scope, and we give you an estimate for larger work after the first conversation.'
      - q: How do we get started?
        a: Book a call or send a message through the contact page. We reply within [RESPONSE_TIME] and suggest a sensible first step.
      - q: Can you work alongside our own team?
        a: Yes. Many clients keep ownership of some systems and ask us to cover the rest. We document as we go so your team can take over.
      - q: Do you offer support after delivery?
        a: Yes. We can run the platform as a managed service with an SLA, or hand it over with documentation and sessions with your engineers.
      - q: How are projects priced?
        a: '[PRICING_MODELS] We agree commercial terms in writing before work starts.'
  - title: Security, data and compliance
    items:
      - q: Can you keep our data in the EU?
        a: We design for EU regions and document where data lives and who can access it. If a requirement cannot be met, we say so plainly.
      - q: Will you certify us for GDPR, NIS2 or DORA?
        a: No. We prepare you for the requirements, run gap analyses and help you gather evidence. Certification and legal advice sit with the relevant bodies and your own counsel.
      - q: How do you handle access to our environments?
        a: We agree access in writing before work starts and aim to use least-privilege roles through your own identity provider.
  - title: AI and agentic AI
    items:
      - q: Can you customise GenAI and agentic solutions for our business?
        a: Yes. We start from a specific use case and shape the solution around your workflows, data sources and regulatory needs, including where people stay in the loop.
      - q: Which AI platforms do you use?
        a: AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI and Google Vertex AI, chosen to fit your cloud and data requirements.
      - q: How do you know an AI application works?
        a: We set up evaluation from the start, so quality can be measured and improved over time instead of judged by impressions.
      - q: Do you help with the EU AI Act?
        a: We help you set up AI governance and prepare for the requirements. This is readiness support, not legal advice.
