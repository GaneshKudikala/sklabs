## What we collect

- **Contact form.** If you use the contact form we receive your name, company, email address, service interest, budget range and message. We use them only to reply to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (and steps you ask us to take before a contract).
- **Website analytics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track individuals across sites or build profiles.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider processes technical data such as IP address and requests to deliver the site and keep it secure.

## What we do not do

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not store form submissions in a database. Messages are delivered to us by email.
